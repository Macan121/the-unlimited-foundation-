About the New Frontier

Preparing a generation that understands the systems it hopes to lead.

The New Frontier Africa is a youth leadership development platform under The Unlimited Foundation. We bring emerging leaders closer to the people, institutions and ideas shaping Africa's future.

Starting with our inaugural summit in Freetown, we connect ambition with practical understanding across public service, enterprise, technology and society.
